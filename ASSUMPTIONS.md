| A-23 | **Duplicate photos are detected by a 16×16 normalised greyscale fingerprint** (mean absolute difference). At ≤ 0.22 a photo is the same and is skipped at ingest; at ≤ 0.30 it is flagged for a look. Measured on the 162 site photos: re-saved copies ≤ 0.11, 2%-cropped copies ≤ 0.19, two different photos ≥ 0.31. A 64-bit dHash was tried first and missed 55/162 re-saved copies. | Agnes may re-send photos that are already on the site, and "don't make duplicates" was the instruction. | `pipeline/lib/fingerprint.mjs`, `INGEST.dupMax/similarMax` in `pipeline/00-ingest.mjs` | `npm run eval:ingest` (the dedupe table: recall on copies, closest different pair) | Heavier crops or recolours are not caught.
